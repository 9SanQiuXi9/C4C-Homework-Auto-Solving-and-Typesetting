# Comprehensive Calculus Worksheet: Limits, Derivatives, and Integration

## Problems

1. Suppose $\delta$ is a positive real number. How do you describe all real numbers $x$ that are within $\delta$ of 0?

(a) Using inequalities ($<, >$).
(b) Using absolute value notation.
(c) Using interval notation.

2. Suppose $a$ is a real number and $\delta$ is a positive real number. How do you describe all real numbers $x$ that are within $\delta$ of $a$, but not equal to $a$:

(a) Graphically.
(b) Using inequalities.
(c) Using absolute value notation.
(d) Using interval notation.

3. Let $f(x) = x^2$.

(a) Find all the positive numbers $x$ such that $f(x)$ is within 1 of 9.
(b) Find a real number $\delta$ such that whenever $x$ is within $\delta$ of 3, $f(x)$ is within 1 of 9.
(c) Find a real number $\delta$ such that whenever $x$ is within $\delta$ of 3, $f(x)$ is within $1/2$ of 9.
(d) Is it true that for any positive number $\varepsilon$, there is a positive $\delta$ so that $f(x)$ is within $\varepsilon$ of 9 whenever $x$ is within $\delta$ of 3?

4. Graph $y = 2x - x^2$. For which points $a$ is the tangent line to the curve at the point $(a, 2a - a^2)$ a horizontal line? How is the value of $2x - x^2$ at these points related to the values of $2x - x^2$ at other points?

5. The point $(2, 0)$ is on the curve $y = 2x - x^2$.

(a) Draw the tangent line to the curve at the point $(2, 0)$.
(b) Try to determine the equation of this tangent line.

6. (a) Find $\lim_{x \to 0^+}\left(\frac{1}{x} - \frac{1}{|x|}\right)$, $\lim_{x \to 0^-}\left(\frac{1}{x} - \frac{1}{|x|}\right)$, and $\lim_{x \to 0}\left(\frac{1}{x} - \frac{1}{|x|}\right)$, if they exist.

(b) Show that $\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{|x|}{x}$ does not exist.

7. Below are listed three limits. Match the best method to each limit and evaluate each limit.

(a) $\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{x^{1000} - 703x^{506} + \pi x^{17} + 480}{-372x^{75} + 39x^{14} + \sqrt{7}x^5 - 24}$

(b) $\lim_{x \to 4} \frac{x^2 + 2x - 24}{x - 4}$

(c) $\lim_{x \to 0} x^2 \sin\left(\frac{1}{x}\right)$

8. Using a calculator, try to guess $\lim_{n \to \infty} n \sin\left(\frac{\pi}{n}\right)$. (Remember that $n$ is in radians, not degrees!)

9. A wine barrel of height $h$ and maximum radius $R$ is formed by rotating the parabola $y = R - cx^2$ (for $-h/2 \le x \le h/2$) around the $x$-axis, where $c > 0$.

(a) Find the radius $r$ of the barrel at its ends (at $x = \pm h/2$). Express your answer in terms of $R$, $c$, and $h$.
(b) Compute the volume of the barrel using the disk method: $V = \pi \int_{-h/2}^{h/2} (R - cx^2)^2 \, dx$.

10. Draw a circle of radius 1. Inscribe a regular $n$-gon inside it.

(a) What is the measure of the angles formed around the center of the circle?
(b) What is the area of one triangle? What is the area of the polygon?
(c) Calculate $\lim_{n \to \infty}$ (Area of $n$-gon). Does this answer make sense intuitively?
